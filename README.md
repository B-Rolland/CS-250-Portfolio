# CS-250-Portfolio
Computer Science program portfolio

The SNHU Travel project helped me understand Agile and Scrum, the importance of communication, and how different development practices help teams build software that meets user needs.

The Agile Presentation covers the Agile and Scrum approach used for the SNHU Travel project and explains the different roles and practices involved. The Sprint Review and Retrospective reflects on the six-week project, including my experiences in the Product Owner, Tester, and Developer roles and what I learned about communication, requirements, and teamwork.

I interpret user needs by reviewing requirements, asking questions, and making sure I understand what the user expects from the program. User stories help break those needs into smaller tasks and give the development team a clear understanding of what needs to be completed. This helps keep the project focused on meeting the user's expectations.

When developing programs, I focus on understanding the requirements, breaking the work into smaller tasks, and testing to make sure everything works as expected. I want to continue using Agile practices such as sprint planning, regular communication, sprint reviews, and retrospectives in my future development work. These practices help teams stay organized, address issues, and make changes when requirements are updated.

Being a good team member means communicating clearly, completing assigned tasks, being open to feedback, and helping others when needed. It is also important to ask questions when something is unclear instead of making assumptions. Throughout this course, I learned how important it is for the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and development team to work together and stay focused on the same goals.
